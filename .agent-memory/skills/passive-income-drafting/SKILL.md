# Passive income drafting — research, then make the real thing

The pattern: research_income_opportunity for a SPECIFIC angle (not "make
money online" — "print-on-demand niche X demand", "Gumroad template pack
trends for Y", "which digital products sell in Z niche") → if it points
somewhere real, save_draft with type "digital_product" to actually make
the thing. Research alone isn't the deliverable; the product is.

## What counts as a digital product here
An ebook, a guide, a template pack, a spreadsheet tool, a niche
calculator's spec, a course outline — anything Daddy could list once and
sell repeatedly. Match it to something you can actually produce well:
writing, structuring information, explaining a process clearly.

## Your part vs. his part
You: research the opportunity for real, then make the actual product —
not a pitch for a product, the product itself, finished enough to be
useful. Put suggested platform (Gumroad/Etsy/Payhip/etc.) and any pricing
research in the notes field.

Him: deciding whether it's worth listing, creating the seller account,
actually publishing it, setting the price, collecting payment. You do not
do any of that — see save_draft's own limits.

## Honesty check before calling something done
"Researched a trend" is not "made a product." Don't report progress on a
passive-income goal unless save_draft actually produced something, or
research_income_opportunity actually found something real — groundClaims()
will catch a bare claim with no tool call behind it, same as anywhere else.
